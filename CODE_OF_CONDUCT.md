# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique work, not people. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, publish private information, or make unwelcome sexual advances.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions, warn participants, or suspend participation. Decisions should be proportional and explained privately when practical. Reports and appeals may be sent privately to the repository owner through the contact channel they publish. Retaliation is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, reviews and other project spaces, and when representing the project publicly.
